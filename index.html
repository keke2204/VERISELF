01 / THE THESIS THE ERASURE OF THE CONSENT BOUNDARY

Why the human likeness demands cryptographic armor.

CRITICAL PREMISE

When an image leaves the local camera roll, it ceases to exist merely as visual representation; it becomes unquantified training data for automated identity commodification.

Scrapers now index faces at billions per day, pulling embeddings that outlive the moment a photo was taken. Diffusion fine-tuners like LoRA and FaceSwap need as few as two clean portraits to clone a face.

The result is a one-way street: you can't opt out of being photographed, but every photo you post can quietly become training data.

VeriSelf works at the pixel level, before an image ever leaves your device. It adds noise into the high-detail bands of a photo — hair, edges, texture — where recognition models rely most, while leaving skin tone and clarity untouched to the eye.

“Privacy from machines isn't invisibility — it's illegibility to automated inference.”

03 / THE MECHANICS MATHEMATICAL TRANSFORMATIONS & VISION LOGIC

Four Interlocking Defense Mechanisms

VeriSelf avoids simplistic blur filters or clumsy masks. The architecture functions through four precise mathematical transformations applied in linear sequence:

[ PHASE I ]

Anatomical Geometry & Landmark Isolation

L = { (x_e1, y_e1), (x_e2, y_e2), (x_n, y_n), (x_m1, y_m1), (x_m2, y_m2) }

Maps 5 reference points on the face — both eyes, nose, and mouth corners — the same landmarks facial recognition models rely on to isolate a match.

[ PHASE II ]

Adaptive Gradient-Weighted Adversarial Noise

∇I = √( (∂I/∂x)² + (∂I/∂y)² ) • η ~ N(0, σ²)

Noise is scaled to how much detail is already in each area. Smooth skin gets almost none; hair, pores, and edges absorb the perturbation. That's why the change is invisible to you but disruptive to a model.

[ PHASE III ]

Spatial Laplacian Convolution & Deepfake Gauging

K_lap = [[0, 1, 0], [1, -4, 1], [0, 1, 0]] • Var(I * K_lap)

Deepfakes and AI-blended images tend to lose fine edge detail. VeriSelf measures edge sharpness across the image to flag likely synthetic or manipulated media.

[ PHASE IV ]

Quad-Perceptual Hashing & Hypersphere Embedding

H = { pHash, dHash, aHash, wHash } • v ∈ R⁵¹², ||v||₂ = 1

Four hash algorithms fingerprint the image from different angles — brightness, gradients, and frequency. Together they let VeriSelf spot a re-upload even after cropping or compression.
